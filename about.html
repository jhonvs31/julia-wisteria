Our Journey

Crafted with Purpose

Julia Wisteria began as a simple idea — that a gift should feel like an embrace. Born in Orlando, Florida, our story is one of passion, artisanship, and the belief that every moment deserves to be celebrated beautifully.

Our Mission

More Than a Gift Box

We exist to make people feel seen, celebrated, and loved — through the art of thoughtful gifting. Every ribbon tied, every item curated, every box sealed with intention is our way of helping you speak when words fall short.

Orlando, Florida
Meet the Founder

Julia's Vision

Julia grew up surrounded by the warmth of handmade things — her grandmother's kitchen, her mother's garden, the artisan markets of downtown Orlando. When she began creating gift boxes in 2020, it wasn't a business plan. It was a love language made tangible.

Today, Julia Wisteria is a reflection of everything she believed a gift could be: intentional, beautiful, and deeply personal. Every artisan partner is chosen by Julia herself, every product tested at her own table, every box approved before it leaves the studio. Her standard is simple — if she wouldn't be moved to receive it, it doesn't ship.

Based in Orlando, Florida, Julia's mission is to keep gifting local, keep it artisan, and keep it extraordinary — one perfectly tied ribbon at a time.

Then & Now

Our Journey

From a kitchen table in Orlando to gifting over 500 families

2020

The First Box

Julia assembles the very first gift box in her Orlando kitchen, gifting it to a friend who had just gone through a difficult month. The response changes everything — three orders follow within a week, all from word of mouth alone.

2021

Going Official

Julia Wisteria is born as a formal business. The first 50 orders are hand-delivered across Orlando in Julia's own car, each box accompanied by a personally written card. The brand's voice — warm, artisan, intentional — is established from day one.

2022

Corporate Discovery

Local businesses begin requesting custom branded boxes for client gifts and employee milestones. Corporate gifting becomes a pillar of the brand, opening a new chapter of scale without ever sacrificing the handmade touch.

2024

Growing Family

The team expands to 8 artisans — each a specialist in their craft, each sharing Julia's obsession with quality. Over 500 boxes delivered. Still every box made with the same love as the first one assembled on that kitchen table.
